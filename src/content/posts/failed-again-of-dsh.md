I think I am switching back to the terminal again. "Everything in the terminal" still seems to be what I want. A GUI is better for viewing than a terminal, but I constantly want to edit files and do other manual work.

## Staying in the Terminal

For years I have built a lot of workflows and setups in the terminal. I am comfortable with it, it is kind of my comfort zone, and I have decided to stay with it. I don't have many comfortable things in life; stepping out of this comfort zone is a pass.

I even looked up what DHH uses in his daily life and work. It was a surprise and a relief that he still mainly stays in the terminal too; that healed my FOMO.

## About DeepSeek Harness

I think DeepSeek Harness is a good way to build or customize personal and team workflows and products. The DeepSeek team is targeting most people, not just developers, and turning DeepSeek Harness into an AI agent dashboard may be a very good use and orientation. You can build whatever you want, and the everything-is-a-plugin design is quite elegant.

I will still keep following the progress of DSH, and I want to build something on it. DSH is still the future.
